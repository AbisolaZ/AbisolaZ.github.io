01 / RESEARCH

From nutrient perturbation to mechanism

My doctoral work asks how cancer cells rewire metabolism under alanine stress, and which responses represent useful therapeutic liabilities rather than secondary effects of slowed growth.

Current research question

What makes alanine metabolism a tractable vulnerability in pancreatic cancer?

I use inducible metabolic perturbation alongside nutrient and inhibitor studies, biochemical assays and longitudinal growth experiments.

This work tests how pancreatic cancer cells adapt to nutrient stress and which responses are functionally linked to proliferation. Mechanistic findings are being reserved for manuscripts in preparation.
